# Guidelines for Using AI Tools

AI tools may support the preparation of issues and merge requests, but they do
not replace the responsibility of the person submitting them. Contributors are
accountable for the quality and content of their submissions,
regardless of whether AI was involved.

Generative AI can help produce content quickly, but good contributions still
depend on human judgment, critical thinking, and careful review. We value
high-quality code, clear and accurate documentation, and focused merge requests
that avoid unnecessary changes or code churn.

## Principles

Whether or not AI tools are used, contributors should:

- Confirm that the proposed change is necessary.
- Keep changes minimal, focused, and appropriately scoped.
- Follow the project's existing coding styles, conventions, and patterns.
- Add tests that properly exercise the change.

## Reviewing AI-Generated Work

Authors must thoroughly review any work produced with the assistance of AI
before submitting an issue or merge request. The author is responsible for
ensuring that the result is correct, relevant, maintainable, and consistent
with the project.

**Contributors should be able to explain their proposed changes in their own
words, including the reasoning behind them.**

Disclosing the use of AI tools in a merge request description is appreciated,
although it is not required.

## Use cases

Generative AI may be used to support activities such as:

- Writing or improving comments, particularly when English is not the
  contributor's first language.
- Developing a better understanding of existing code.
- Supplementing a contributor's knowledge when working on code, tests, or
  documentation.

## Maintainers

Maintainers may close issues and merge requests that are unhelpful,
unproductive, or unsuitable for the project, regardless of whether AI tools
were used.
